# Sage demo video: shot list

Goal: a 45–60 second screen recording that makes people stop scrolling. Show, don't explain. Record with **Cmd + Shift + 5**, record your mic too, and set Sage's voice to **ElevenLabs** for the take.

## Before recording

- Download a Premium Mac voice as a backup (System Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content).
- Clean desktop, hide personal notifications (Focus → Do Not Disturb).
- Use apps people recognize: Figma, Final Cut, Photoshop, Blender, VS Code, Excel.
- Do 3–4 practice runs per shot and keep the best one. Claude's answers vary.

## Shots

**1. Hook (0–5s).** Open on a busy, intimidating app (Blender or Final Cut works well). Text overlay: *"I built an AI that can see my screen and point at things."*

**2. The tour (5–20s).** Hold Control + Option: *"Give me a quick tour of this app."*
The cursor hops from panel to panel as Sage explains each one. This is the money shot. Don't cut it.

**3. Find the button (20–32s).** In Figma or Photoshop: *"How do I export this as a PNG?"*
Sage flies straight to the export control while it talks.

**4. Something useful (32–45s).** In VS Code with a visible error, or a suspicious email: *"What's wrong here?"*
Sage points at the exact line or link.

**5. Close (45–55s).** Text overlay: *"Sage. Early access at kesideonyeocha.com/sage"* while the cursor flies back to your mouse.
