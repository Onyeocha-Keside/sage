Privacy & your data

Only when you ask.

Questions and screenshots

Sage captures your displays after you ask. During follow-along, it captures the current screen again after your actions to guide the next step. Your questions and screenshots are processed through Sage’s Cloudflare Worker and Anthropic’s Claude.

Sage has no analytics or ads, does not sell your data, and does not save screenshots to disk.

Voice

On-device voice recognition depends on your Mac and the available language support; Apple’s speech service may be used when it is unavailable. The optional ElevenLabs voice sends response text to ElevenLabs to produce audio. The Mac voice uses your computer’s speech synthesizer.

The waitlist

We store your email address, chosen platform, signup dates, and website origin in Cloudflare storage for early-access updates. To request removal, contact the creator through their website.

Provider processing is subject to each provider’s data policies.
